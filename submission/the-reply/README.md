# The Reply

**Video:** [`the-reply.mp4`](the-reply.mp4), 2:41. The response to Steph's answer to
[The Million-Dollar Question](../million-dollar-question/).

## What it shows

- **The books first.** Agnolo reads the day's ledger before anyone opens the letter: 8 h 18 min from
  the first cut to the last, 9 films, 17:14 of picture. Muse Code 23 commits, Muse Spark 4 live calls,
  230 voice lines, and every decision Leo's. Full timeline: [`../LEDGER.md`](../LEDGER.md).
- **Steph's reply, read on air.** The monitor shows his public reply in full. Borges reads the three
  values he named:
  - an introduction to the right person at the right time;
  - a hundred more videos at 1,000 to 10,000 views each;
  - a vote of confidence that changed a life.
- **The cast's interpretation.**
  - Borges calls the three values a door, a future and a meaning.
  - Agnolo notices the second one is the million again: up to 10,000 views on each of 100 films.
- **Predictions of Leo's answers to the three questions in his post.** They are labelled as
  predictions on screen and in the dialogue.

  | Question | Agnolo | Borges | Leonardo |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Is the tweet worth $1M? | Not yet. Nothing has been paid. | Already, to one person. | Yes |
  | Can it get 1M views? | Not this tweet. Maybe the series. | | Yes |
  | Does it show what's possible with the winning entry? | | Yes: nine films in one day. | Yes |

- **The answer to Steph.**
  - A meaning: whether a vote of confidence changed a life is Leo's to say, not the cast's.
  - A future: they can't promise a hundred videos, but they can show the rate.
  - A door: who is the right person? Tell us, and the next film is for them.
