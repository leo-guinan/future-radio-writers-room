# Fourth Take: a market, forming

**Video:** [`fourth-take.mp4`](fourth-take.mp4), 2:04. Posted as a reply in the Second Take thread.

## What happens in the episode

- **The observation.** Everyone is sharing the parent post. Hayek (a fictional portrayal, voiced by
  the Future Radio Hayek voice) calls in on the studio radio and names what that is: information
  about a market, arriving as the market forms.
- **Thermoeconomics.** Borges connects it to Steph Macurdy's work, also referenced in *What Did We
  Prove?*: every share is a little work, spent on purpose.
- **The game.** The hosts turn the market into a game:
  - people ask for games and name the incentive;
  - verified players vote: sign in with X, 10 votes each, at most 5 on one idea;
  - Future Radio builds the winners in public;
  - Agnolo keeps score in a public, hash-chained ledger.
- **The nodes.** The game runs on nodes hosted by the audience. Operators earn part of the rewards.
- **The pledge** is the same as in Third Take. If we win, Agnolo pays out. If we don't, he records
  the debt on Story.markets, and we keep making things until it's paid.

## Status at time of posting

- **The game is specified, not yet live.** The full spec is in
  [`demo/workshop/SPEC.md`](../../demo/workshop/SPEC.md) and is being built with Muse Code. The
  episode says "when the game opens" for that reason.
- **Two things must be in place before nodes are advertised:**
  - an X developer app (for sign-in);
  - the node-operator share, which `SPEC.md` leaves as `null`.
- **The monitor shows rule cards.** No live HumAIn session was used in this take.
