# The Lab That Already Exists

**Video:** [`the-lab-that-already-exists.mp4`](the-lab-that-already-exists.mp4), 14:20. An episode about Annalea Layton
(@annalea_l), solo founder of Vext Labs, building JUWEL ([vextlabs.ai](https://vextlabs.ai/)).

## What it shows

- **The wrong rabbit hole.** Leonardo wants to give her a HumAIn OS laptop. She says she already has essentially unlimited
  compute, is exhausted by the work of being a founder, and would rather work for an AI lab. The browser opens on
  vextlabs.ai: she already runs one.
- **A million people.** She reports a million people saw her work in two days. Agnolo won't book it: reach isn't revenue,
  revenue isn't profit, and neither is a person's whole contribution.
- **The researcher.** Six publicly listed papers, each with a DOI. Her research looks at adding capabilities to a model while
  preserving the ones it has, with explicit checks. HumAIn OS works on related questions at a different layer.
- **The foresight.**
  - *The Accretion Model*: a model that grows by adding new parts, never by changing old ones.
  - Verifier-gated improvement and leakage. EVAL shouts.
  - Ideas that failed stay on her list, and one paper is a record of them.
- **The frontier is not just a model.** JUWEL: what the site lists as live, and what it lists as not shipped or still a draft.
  Agnolo stops a partnership before anyone invents one.
- **The world outside the lab.** She says her house has flooded. Unlimited compute doesn't help with that.
- **The Artup.** Mrs. Bus asks whether she wants a cofounder. Nobody knows, so they've found a question, not a partnership.
- **The million-dollar question.** Agnolo asks who she's worth a million to. The views aren't the price; they're what made
  the cast look.
- **The invitation, not the rescue.** Mrs. Bus drafts the dispatch, and the door opens from the inside.
- **The research that compounds.** Her research asks how a machine can gain new capabilities without losing its past. The
  cast want to help build a world where the human doesn't lose hers.
- **The dispatch.**
  - Partnership status: not established.
  - Sponsor commitments: none.
- **Post-credits.** The cofounder interview has one question: can you reliably reduce the founder's workload?
