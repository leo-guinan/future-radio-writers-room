# Take Eight: who are the real referees?

**Video:** [`take-eight.mp4`](take-eight.mp4), 1:19.

## What it shows

- **The opening is the honest hook EVAL asked for in Take Seven:** "We rewrote this show seven times
  today. Now we need referees."
- **A real referee and a pretender.** Two would-be referees write in. Referee A has posted its
  fingerprint in the thread, from the linked X account, and passed its last audit: **authorized**.
  Referee B has no fingerprint and no entry in the registry: **not authorized**.
- **Anyone can check.** The referee list is signed, its key is pinned in the first post, and any node
  answers `check_referee`.
- **What each side earns.** The pretender earns nothing for the people they recruit, though those
  people still play. Real referees earn for catching pretenders.

## Notes

- **Referees A and B are a labelled demonstration,** not real accounts; the card and the end card say
  so. The registry is specified (`demo/workshop/SPEC.md`, v6) and is being built with Muse Code. It
  isn't live yet.
