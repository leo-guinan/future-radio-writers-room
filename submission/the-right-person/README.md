# The Right Person

**Video:** [`the-right-person.mp4`](the-right-person.mp4), 2:29. Steph answers the question from
[The Reply](../the-reply/): "One right person is @NJHagens."

## What it shows

- **Look him up first.** Agnolo insists the cast learn who Nate Hagens is before asking him for
  anything. Borges reads his public bio:
  - host of *The Great Simplification* and author of *Reality Blind*;
  - ten years in finance (Salomon Brothers, Lehman Brothers) before leaving Wall Street to study
    energy, ecology and economics;
  - former editor of *The Oil Drum*;
  - director of the Institute for the Study of Energy & Our Future;
  - teacher of *Reality 101* at the University of Minnesota.
- **Energy blind.** Borges sums up his argument in his own paraphrase, labelled on screen as a
  paraphrase and not a quote: the economy runs on energy, and money is a claim on it.
- **Honest about our own game.** Leonardo connects it to the workshop's energy-bound nodes. Agnolo
  stops him: the ledger records the watts and source each operator declares, but doesn't verify
  them. "He would notice." The monitor shows what is checked and what isn't
  (`demo/workshop/EXPERIMENT.md`).
- **What he could help with, if he wanted to:**
  - a reality check on whether an energy receipt means anything;
  - listeners who already think in energy;
  - a question: can creativity buy compute without spending the future?
- **The test**, taken from his own bio: is the game better than the default, or just louder?
- **The door.**
  - The next film is for him, as promised.
  - Steph pointed, and the introduction is Steph's to make.
  - The cast have never met him, and he owes them nothing.
