# The prompt

This is the request that started the episode, as Leo gave it to Claude on Nov 5, 2026, with the three screenshots that came
with it (in [`prompt/`](prompt/)).

> Now let's make a new movie. one that recognizes jessyka for her recommendation of annalea as a possible cofounder. pull the
> history with her from the archive. the movie should outline taking the production process for future radio and making it a
> hermes plugin. the acceptance test is producing the episode we create to the exact specs with the plugin. the test for her
> swarm: can she reverse engineer the movie given the prompt and the architecture? let's create a repo for her as a starter,
> let her fork it, and see what she can do with it.

## "The archive"

"The archive" is the [Community Archive](https://www.community-archive.org/), an open database of uploaded Twitter/X
archives. Leo's archive is in it; Jessyka's isn't, so only Leo's side of their history can be read there.
