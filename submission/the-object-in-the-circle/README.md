# The Object in the Circle

**Video:** [`the-object-in-the-circle.mp4`](the-object-in-the-circle.mp4), 5:54. Vibecamp is awarded a $300k grant from Lightcone Commons.
Leonardo tries to claim the credit. The cast celebrate the people who earned it.

## What it shows

- **The claim.** Mrs. Bus brings Brooke Bowman's announcement. Leonardo points to Leo's quote post ("Arm the artists with money
  and let them cook") and claims the artists are armed because of it.
- **The timestamps.** Agnolo checks the dates:
  - Brooke's post: Nov 2, 2:52 PM.
  - Leo's quote post: Nov 4, 11:16 AM, 44 hours later.

  The grant came from Lightcone Commons and the people who contributed to it. The Vibecamp team earned it over years, sometimes
  pausing their own small paychecks so the organization could pay its bills.
- **Leo's own Vibecamp.** Borges reads Leo's retrospective,
  [What I Learned From Spending 4 Days In the Woods With A Bunch of Internet Weirdos](https://engineeringgenerosity.substack.com/p/what-i-learned-from-spending-4-days)
  (Jul 26, 2025):
  - Leo's ticket was itself a grant, from Defender and the Open Research Institute ("before anyone armed the artists, somebody
    armed Leo");
  - the opening session practised saying and accepting yes, saying and accepting no, and responding graciously;
  - the improv circle passed an object that exists only in the minds of the people in the circle.
- **Credit is the object in the circle.** If you grab it and hold on to it, the game stops; pass it on and it comes back bigger.
  Defender's post: "no one really wants to save the world, what we want is to be given a role in saving it."
- **The celebration.** What Brooke says the money is for:
  - paying people enough that they don't risk burnout;
  - training budgets, especially for the safety council;
  - breathing room.

  The financial report they had already started makes Agnolo cry.
- **The ledger.** Credit claimed by Future Radio: none. Joy: unlimited.
- **The reply.** "Thank you, Lightcone Commons. Congratulations, Vibecamp." Post-credits, Leonardo asks for a footnote in the
  financial report. Denied.

## Notes

- Brooke Bowman doesn't speak in the film. Her words are quoted from her public posts (Nov 2, 2026), and the monitor shows
  her post and Leo's quote post as screenshots.
- Future Radio had nothing to do with the grant.
- Leo's experience comes from his own retrospective. Defender's post is quoted there.
