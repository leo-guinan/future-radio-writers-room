# The Million-Dollar Question

**Video:** [`million-dollar-question.mp4`](million-dollar-question.mp4), 2:06.

**The hook:** is a tweet with a million views worth a million dollars?

## What it shows

- **Views are not value.** A counter rolls to 1,000,000. Borges: "A million people looking at
  something is not the same as one person finding it valuable."
- **The trust subscription.** Agnolo opens the Story.markets ledger:
  - Steph Macurdy, $168.00, reported cumulative payments.
  - His support began before the hackathon, so before this movie existed.
  - Paying before the work exists is a trust subscription. "Then he wasn't paying for the movie!"
- **The question.** Leonardo asks Steph: what information, at exactly the right moment, would be
  worth one million dollars to you, and what would you do with it?
  - Not because he would pay that much, but because of what he could understand, discover or create
    after receiving it.
  - His earlier payments create no obligation to answer.
- **The demo behind the cast.** While Leonardo asks, the studio monitor shows the live Art Bus
  Workshop page, the coordinator from the `workshop-game` branch running locally, with its countdown
  ticking. The question is typed into its request form as a video request.
- **The invitation.** Mrs. Bus asks where to take the question. "To Steph." The reply window stays
  open, and anyone who watches can answer the same question for themselves.

## Notes

- **The ledger amount is as reported** to us, not pulled live from Story.markets.
- **The question was typed, not submitted.** The workshop requires an X sign-in to submit, and the
  monitor says so on screen. The workshop is not deployed publicly yet.
- **Voices and timing.** Leonardo, Borges, Agnolo and Mrs. Bus are ElevenLabs voices (eleven_v4).
  The episode follows the two-minute production cut, with Leonardo's two closing lines merged into
  one.
