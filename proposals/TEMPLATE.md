## What you want to be able to do

One paragraph, from the point of view of whoever uses it (a patron, the producer, a character,
another room).

## Why it matters

What it would change for the show, its patrons, or other rooms. Evidence is welcome: an event
id (`ev_000123`), an episode, a cut, a conversation.

## What you already know

What you tried, what you think it touches (files, layers), and what you don't know how to do.

## Done when

The stopping rule: how anyone would tell it works.
