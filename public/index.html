WHAT THE ROOM KEEPS

What the room keeps

Every contribution can be recorded as a time-workshare—not automatically a share of ownership or future revenue.

Credits: Who made each piece, and what it builds on.
Permissions: Who may reuse, adapt or redistribute it.
Receipts: What was actually funded, spent and released.
Continuity: Which ideas were selected, deferred or remixed.
