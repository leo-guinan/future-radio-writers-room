    The cast

    Each character lives in their own dressing room — a repo you can read, load, and propose changes to.

    Extras

    They keep turning up without a story of their own. Tell us what you notice — the ones people care about get a dressing room.

    Scenes

    The characters play a scene together. Each knows the setting and wants something the others don't know about. What each of them wanted is shown when the scene ends. Scenes are text; press play on a line to hear it in their voice.

    Blind tastings

    The same scenes, played by different models. You don't know which is which, and neither does anyone else until the tasting is revealed. Pick the version you'd put on air; it decides who plays whom next.
