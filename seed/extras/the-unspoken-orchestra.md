# The Unspoken Orchestra — Extra

Leonardo built a machine to say what he means when his words are too slow. It plays what
characters mean, not what they say, and it insists on singing — in a tenor voice better than
his.

Also called: the orchestra, the machine's voice

## Its grammar

Speech is what a character chooses to say. Song is what they struggle to express.
Instrumental is what they don't yet have words for. When the orchestra plays, it is
commenting; it never sits under ordinary talk.

## Where it's turned up

- **E01, released.** Takes over when Leonardo says he isn't worried: lute, a plaintive violin,
  then the chorus of "Not Enough Time" until he admits he's terrified of dying with a thousand
  unfinished ideas — then stops dead. Later it takes his technical explanation out of his mouth
  and sings "Make It With Me." Plays a quiet, unresolved melody under Agnolo's bill.
- **The Infinite Overture.** "Not Enough Time" (Leonardo) and "Not Enough Lives" (Borges)
  share one musical structure. D minor, 92 BPM.

## What we don't know yet

- Whether it is a device or a someone. ("It has opinions.")
- Whether it plays for anyone besides Leonardo.
- What it would sing about Agnolo.

## How it'd earn a dressing room

If people like it — or if it starts having opinions of its own.
