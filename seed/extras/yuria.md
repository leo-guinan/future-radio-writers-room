# Yuria — Extra

A modern voice on the future side of the radio. She keeps turning up, and nobody has told
her story yet.

Also called: the listener in the static, the announcer

## Where she's turned up

- **E01, released.** Credited as "the listener in the static." Leonardo's machine catches her
  mid-sentence, talking to someone in her own room: "—no, leave it on. I like this one." It is
  his first proof that someone is there.
- **The Infinite Overture.** The same voice says "That's the one I want to see" in the earlier
  film-opening work (per the Episode 001 production doc's cast table).
- **E10, planned, not released.** Announces Episode 10 as the second broadcast (scene E10-02),
  and invites a listener to describe a song that doesn't exist yet (E10-15).

## What we don't know yet

- Whether the listener in E01 and the announcer in E10 are the same person, or one voice
  playing two people.
- Who she was talking to, and what made her leave it on.
- When she is.
- Whether she knows she's on the radio.

## How she'd earn a dressing room

If people like her: more appearances, viewer notes about her, ideas about her that people vote
for. The producer decides, and her history moves with her.
