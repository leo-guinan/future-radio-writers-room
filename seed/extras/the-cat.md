# The cat — Extra

Leonardo's cat, in the workshop in Florence, 1504. She never speaks: she purrs and meows.

Also called: the research cat

## Where she's turned up

- **E01, released.** Purring in the workshop when the signal locks. Meows on cue for "The
  beeswax was your idea." Purrs after the orchestra stops. Meows right after the listener in
  the static says "I like this one."

## What we don't know yet

- Her name.
- Whether she can hear the future, or just likes the hum.
- What the research is.

## How she'd earn a dressing room

If people like her. A cat with no lines can still carry a scene.
