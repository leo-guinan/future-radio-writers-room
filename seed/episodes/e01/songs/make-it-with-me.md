# "Make It With Me"

Sung by the machine in Episode 001, scene 4 — the same tenor voice that sings "Not Enough Time", so listeners learn that voice belongs to the machine. About 1:45; 92 BPM, lifting from D minor into D major. The machine turns Leonardo's technical explanation into an invitation.

Released take: take 2 (92.3 BPM, D minor, word-accurate). Takes 1 and 4 are clean alternates.

**[Verse 1]**
I've got a wing that's waiting for the wind
A bridge that's only halfway to the shore
A page of plans with nowhere to begin
And every plan just asks me for one more

**[Pre-chorus]**
I can draw the gears, I can draw the door
But I can't turn every handle on my own

**[Chorus]**
So make it with me, make it with me
You bring the hours I can't keep
I'll bring the drawings from my sleep
Make it with me, make it with me
Half of a thing is a thing begun
And two half-things can make it one

**[Verse 2]**
Somewhere you're living in a year I'll never see
With hands I'll never shake and songs I'll never know
But if one little spark can travel back to me
I'll build a road where both of us can go

**[Chorus]**

**[Tag, soft, nearly spoken]**
I don't know when you are
But here is where I'll be
If you can hear the signal
Make it with me
